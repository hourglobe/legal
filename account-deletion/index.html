Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Hourglobe account and associated personal data across shophourglobe.com and the Hourglobe mobile apps (Android & iOS), published by Murat Gülmez.

  • Method 1 — Email Request: Send an email to hello@shophourglobe.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Delete my account and data" (please include your order number if contacting us from a different email address).
  • Method 2 — In-App Request: In the Hourglobe mobile app, navigate to My account → Delete my account and data.
  • Method 3 — Online Request Form: Use our request form at shophourglobe.com/pages/delete-account: enter the email address of your account, add your order number if you have one, tick the confirmation box and send. You do not need to email us separately.
  • Verification & Processing Timeline: We acknowledge your request within 7 calendar days and complete permanent deletion within 30 calendar days. For your security, we will never request your account password, one-time verification codes, or full payment card numbers.
  • Data Permanently Deleted: Your customer account profile, saved shipping/billing addresses, phone number, newsletter/marketing preferences, and closed customer support conversations.
  • Data Retained Under Legal Obligations: Certain transactional records must be retained by law even after account deletion: (i) order, invoice and tax records (retained for 10 years under mandatory Turkish Tax and Commercial Laws); (ii) payment/refund references (no full card data); and (iii) records required for open chargebacks, fraud prevention or active legal disputes until resolved. Retained records are strictly isolated and never used for marketing.
  • Third-Party Processors & Local Device Data: We relay your deletion request to our third-party processors (including Shopify, our payment provider, and fulfilment partners) where applicable. Data stored locally on your phone (favourites, cart, language) is removed automatically when you uninstall the app or clear app storage in device settings.
