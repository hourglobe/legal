Account and data deletion

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Hourglobe account and personal data. It applies to our website shophourglobe.com and to the Hourglobe app for Android and iPhone, published by Murat Gülmez.

How to ask

  1. Email us at hello@shophourglobe.com from the email address you used for your orders, with the subject "Delete my account and data". If you used a different address, tell us your order number so we can find your data.
  2. In the app: open Settings → Delete my account and data. This opens this page and the same email request.
  3. We confirm that we received your request within 7 days. We may ask a simple question to check that the request comes from you — we will never ask for a password, a one-time code or your full card number.
  4. We complete the deletion within 30 days (or sooner where the law requires), and email you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your customer account, saved addresses and phone number
  • Your newsletter and marketing preferences
  • Support conversations and photos you sent us, once any open request or dispute is closed
  • Greeting card names, messages and recipient email addresses (these are deleted automatically anyway: card links expire after 60 days and recipient emails 30 days after delivery)

What we must keep — and for how long

Some records must be kept by law even after you ask us to delete your data. We keep only what is needed, we do not use it for marketing, and we delete it when the legal period ends:

  • Order and invoice records (order number, items, amounts, billing name and address): for the period required by tax and commercial law
  • Payment and refund references (no full card data — we never see or store it): as long as needed to resolve your request
  • Records needed for an open dispute, chargeback or fraud check: until it is resolved

Data on your device

The app keeps your favourites, cart, chosen delivery country and language only on your phone. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's storage in your phone settings.

Our partners

We pass your deletion request to the service providers that hold your data on our behalf — our e-commerce platform, our payment provider and our fulfilment partners — where they still keep it and the law allows deletion.

Questions

Write to hello@shophourglobe.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority — see our Privacy Policy.
