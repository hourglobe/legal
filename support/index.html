Hourglobe support

Need help with the Hourglobe app or an order? Write to us at hello@shophourglobe.com. We aim to reply within 24 hours. If your question is about an order, please include your order number (for example #1001).

Orders and delivery

  • We currently deliver to the United States, Canada, Australia and the United Arab Emirates.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Tracked shipping is free and included in the price.
  • To see your orders and tracking, open My Account → My orders in the app, or use the link in your shipping confirmation email.
  • If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, email us with your order number and a photo, and we will make it right.

Payment

Checkout takes place on the Hourglobe store's secure web page, opened inside the app. Your card details are never entered into or stored by the app. Cards issued in Türkiye cannot be used for payment at the moment.

Using the app

  • Gift Wheel: choose who the gift is for, the occasion and your budget; the wheel picks a country and suggests three gifts. It is a gift finder, not a game of chance: no prizes, no discounts.
  • Greeting cards: free digital cards are made on your phone. Save the card as an image or share it yourself; the app does not send it to anyone.
  • Favorites and cart are kept on your phone.
  • Sign in: My Account uses a one-time code sent to your email. There is no password.
  • The app is available in English and Turkish (My Account → Language).

Your account and data

To delete your account and personal data, see Account and data deletion. How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.
