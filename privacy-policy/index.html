3. Mobile App & WhatsApp Business Support

The Hourglobe mobile application (iOS and Android) provides access to our online storefront. Your favourites, shopping cart, selected delivery country and language preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. The app contains no third-party advertising networks or behavioural analytics SDKs and does not request access to your device location, contacts or photo library. When you sign in or complete a purchase, you are directed to secure checkout pages operated by Shopify; payment card details are never entered into or stored by the mobile app.

If you choose to contact our customer support via WhatsApp Business (+90 554 264 19 20), your phone number, profile name, message content and any images/videos you send are processed solely to resolve your support request and are transmitted via Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States) infrastructure.

4. Recipients of Personal Data

We share personal data strictly on a need-to-know basis with the following categories of recipients:

  • E-Commerce Platform: Shopify Inc. (Canada / United States / Ireland) for store hosting, checkout and order management.
  • Payment Service Providers: İyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş. (iyzico) for secure payment processing and refunds.

  • Carriers & Customs Authorities: International postal operators, courier companies (such as DHL, FedEx, UPS) and customs/tax authorities where required for cross-border delivery.
  • Communication Providers: Meta Platforms, Inc. (only when you initiate contact via WhatsApp Business) and email delivery infrastructure providers.
  • Professional Advisers & Authorities: Legal counsel, certified public accountants, and competent public authorities when required by law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. International Data Transfers

Because Hourglobe is established in Türkiye and works with international fulfilment partners, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in Türkiye, the United States, Canada, the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and China. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, UK or Türkiye, we implement appropriate safeguards in accordance with GDPR Chapter V, UK GDPR and Article 9 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK), including adequacy decisions, European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), KVKK Standard Contracts, or where applicable for direct cross-border fulfilment, the contractual necessity derogation (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR / Art. 9(6) KVKK) strictly limited to delivering your order.

6. Data Retention Periods

  • Order, invoice, tax and payment/refund records: Retained for 10 years from the end of the calendar year of the transaction in accordance with mandatory Turkish Tax Procedure Law and Commercial Code obligations.
  • Customer support records (email/WhatsApp) and defect photos: Retained for 3 years following closure of the inquiry to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • One-time country/product availability alert emails: Deleted immediately upon sending the requested one-time launch/stock notification (or within 30 days at the latest).
  • Marketing & newsletter subscriptions: Retained until you withdraw consent or click 'Unsubscribe' in any marketing email.
  • Open disputes, chargebacks or fraud investigations: Retained until final settlement or resolution of the dispute, chargeback or regulatory inquiry.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA/CPRA & Global Laws)

Subject to applicable law in your country or state of residence, you have the right to: (a) request access to and a copy of your personal data; (b) request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data; (c) request erasure ('right to be forgotten') of your data; (d) restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests; (e) receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (data portability); (f) withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing; and (g) opt out of the 'sale' or 'sharing' of personal information or targeted advertising under applicable US state privacy laws (such as California CCPA/CPRA). We recognize and honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser opt-out signals where required.

We respond to verified rights requests within 30 days (or 45 days under CCPA/CPRA). We will never ask for your password, security codes or full card number to verify your identity. To delete your account and data, visit legal.shophourglobe.com/account-deletion.
