3. Our mobile app

The Hourglobe app shows the same shop. Your favourites, cart, chosen delivery country and language are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools and does not ask for your location, contacts or photos. If you allow notifications, reminders (for example a saved cart or the last order date for Christmas delivery) are created on your device. When you pay or sign in, you do so on our shop's secure checkout and account pages operated by our e-commerce platform; card details are never entered into or stored by the app.

4. Who receives your data

We share only what is needed with: our e-commerce platform (Shopify); our payment provider; fulfilment and print-on-demand partners located in China, the United States and the European Union, who receive the delivery details needed to make and ship your order; carriers and customs authorities; our professional advisers where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country, including in Türkiye, China and the United States. We use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect it.

6. How long we keep it

Order and invoice records: for the period required by tax and commercial law. Support conversations: as long as needed to resolve your request. Marketing data: until you unsubscribe. Records needed for an open dispute are kept until it is resolved.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (California) — we honour Global Privacy Control signals where required. We answer within the legal time limit (for example one month under the GDPR, which may be extended; 45 days under the CCPA). We will ask you to confirm your identity in a simple, proportionate way and never ask for passwords or full card details. Some records must be kept for legal reasons; we will tell you if so. To delete your account and data, follow the steps on our Account and data deletion page — it works for both the website and the app.

8. Complaints

You can contact us first at hello@shophourglobe.com. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (for example your national authority in the EU, the ICO in the UK) (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

9. Cookies

Essential cookies make the shop work. Analytics and advertising cookies are used only with your consent where the law requires it; you can change your choice at any time by writing to hello@shophourglobe.com. If you reject optional cookies, the shop still works, including your cart and checkout.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time, from the website or from the app (Settings → Delete my account and data). The steps, what we delete and what we must keep for legal reasons are explained on our Account and data deletion page.

11. Children

Our shop is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
